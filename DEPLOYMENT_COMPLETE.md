### 1. ✅ Smart Email Generator
- Generate professional, context-based emails
- Support for tone variations (formal, informal, persuasive)
- Audience adaptation (client, manager, team, colleague)
- Multiple email purposes (meeting request, update, follow-up, feedback, proposal, apology)
- Copy to clipboard and download functionality

- Automatic extraction of key points

- Copy and download options

### 3. ✅ AI Task Planner & Scheduler

- Intelligent task prioritization
- Time blocking and scheduling
- Productivity optimization strategies
- Flexible planning with custom hours
- Time optimization tips and best practices
